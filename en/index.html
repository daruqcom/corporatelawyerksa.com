Why Companies Work With Us

Our practice is limited to corporate and commercial law. We do not take on family law, personal injury, or unrelated litigation, which allows our lawyers to maintain genuine depth in company formation, governance, transactions, and commercial disputes rather than a general practice spread thin across unrelated areas.

We work in both Arabic and English as a matter of course, not as an added service. Board resolutions, commercial contracts, and correspondence with Saudi authorities are prepared to satisfy both an international parent company and a Saudi regulator where both are involved in the same matter.

Confidentiality is treated as a baseline expectation rather than a selling point. Consultations, including exploratory conversations before any engagement is confirmed, are treated as privileged and are not shared beyond the lawyers directly handling the matter.

Every engagement begins with a scoping conversation to confirm what the matter actually requires, followed by a fixed-fee estimate or retainer proposal before any billable work begins.

We act for founders establishing a first Saudi entity, family-owned businesses managing succession and governance, foreign investors structuring market entry under MISA, and established groups with an ongoing commercial presence in the Kingdom.

Most new client relationships start with one specific matter, often forming a new entity or reviewing an existing contract, then broaden over time to cover other parts of the business. We prefer this gradual pattern to selling a full service package the company doesn't yet need at its current stage.

For foreign investors specifically, MISA licensing is a common starting point, but it's rarely the only matter. Most newly entered foreign companies also need a parallel review of their governance structure and their first commercial contracts with local suppliers or customers. We present this full picture from the first consultation rather than addressing each piece in isolation.

Common Questions

Do you work with companies outside Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We regularly advise foreign parent companies and their in-house counsel on Saudi subsidiary matters, coordinated remotely by video call and email.

How are your fees structured?

Defined matters are typically quoted as a fixed fee once the scope is confirmed. Ongoing corporate relationships are more often handled on a retainer basis.

Can you act as local counsel alongside our existing legal team?

Yes. We frequently act as Saudi counsel of record for a specific matter without displacing a client's broader legal relationships elsewhere.

Do you handle disputes as well as transactional work?

Yes. Our commercial litigation practice sits alongside our transactional and advisory work, so the same team can represent you if a matter escalates.

Is the initial consultation confidential?

Yes. Everything discussed, including before any engagement is confirmed, is treated as privileged and confidential.

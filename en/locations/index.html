Business Hubs

Legal support across multiple cities and regions.

We work with companies across Saudi Arabia, with a stronger concentration in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam, the three commercial centers listed below. Each location page covers what we handle locally and how consultations typically work there.

Location primarily affects the convenience of an in-person meeting, not eligibility to work with us. Companies registered anywhere in the Kingdom can engage our team, with most work coordinated remotely regardless of the city where the company is based.

If your activity spans more than one of these three cities, the same team handles the file across locations rather than splitting it between separate local offices, so full knowledge of your matter stays with one team regardless of where the activity is located.

We chose these three cities because they cover the vast majority of our current clients' commercial activity. As our practice grows, we'll add locations only where we build the same depth we maintain in these cities, not simply to appear to have broader geographic coverage than we actually do.

Companies based outside these three cities work with us regularly too. Location pages describe where we have the strongest local presence, not the limits of who we can work with.

If your business spans Riyadh and Jeddah, for example, the same team manages the file across both cities without needing separate coordination with a different local office in each.

Common Questions

Can we work with you if our company is registered outside Riyadh, Jeddah, or Dammam?

Yes, location only affects the convenience of an in-person meeting, and we work with companies registered anywhere in the Kingdom remotely.

Do fees differ based on the company's location?

No, our fee structure is national rather than city-specific, with the only difference being a simple travel consideration if an in-person meeting is needed in a specific city.

Do you plan to add other cities in the future?

We add new locations only when we build the same depth we maintain in our current cities, not simply to expand the list.

Disputes & Litigation

Disputes & Litigation Legal Services

Disputes & Litigation legal services in Saudi Arabia. Riyadh, Jeddah & Dammam.

Commercial relationships sometimes break down into contractual disagreements, disputes between partners, or other commercial claims, requiring representation before commercial courts and arbitration bodies. Mediation and negotiation often run alongside or ahead of formal proceedings, and settling early is frequently the better outcome for both sides.

A few situations come up repeatedly. A commercial counterparty that hasn't met the terms of a supply or distribution contract. A dispute between partners over profit distribution or management authority. An unpaid financial claim that needs court follow-up after amicable collection has failed. Or an arbitration clause in an existing contract that needs to be triggered once a dispute arises.

These disputes fall under the Commercial Courts Law and its implementing regulations, and are heard before the competent commercial court division based on the dispute's value and the parties' location. Where a contract specifies arbitration, the dispute is heard before an arbitration panel under the Saudi Arbitration Law instead of the ordinary courts.

Engagements begin with an honest assessment of the represented party's legal position and a realistic estimate of the chances of success before deciding whether to proceed. In many cases, a negotiated settlement is faster and less costly than full litigation, and we recommend it when the expected outcome from litigation doesn't justify its cost and duration.

We place particular importance on the timing of filing a claim and documenting evidence, since a delay by the harmed party in taking action can weaken its position later even where the underlying right is clear.

When a commercial dispute carries a potential criminal dimension, such as a bounced check or commercial fraud, we coordinate the civil and criminal tracks together to ensure one doesn't weaken the other.

We also help assess the solvency of the opposing party before entering costly litigation, since winning a judgment against an insolvent party doesn't achieve the financial outcome actually sought, an assessment we make explicitly before recommending any lawsuit.

We help draft dispute resolution clauses in new contracts before any disagreement arises, since choosing the competent forum and litigation or arbitration mechanism at this stage provides real clarity if a dispute occurs later.

We give a candid estimate of expected litigation cost against the dispute's value from the outset, so the client decides based on a complete financial picture, not just the legal merits of the claim.

We've noticed from cases handled across Riyadh and Jeddah's commercial courts that success depends heavily on the quality of everyday commercial correspondence, not just the strength of legal theory alone. Companies that keep organized records of their communications and financial claims are in a far stronger position when a dispute needs to go to court, while companies relying on phone calls or informal verbal understandings struggle to prove their position even when the underlying right is clear. When a dispute involves a contract with an arbitration clause, that path deserves review before heading straight to the commercial court, and disputes carrying a potential criminal dimension, like a bounced check or suspected commercial fraud, benefit from coordinating with our white-collar crime team to cover both angles together.

Common Questions

When is arbitration a suitable alternative to litigation before the commercial court?

When the contract specifies an arbitration clause, or when both parties prefer to keep the dispute confidential and move faster than the ordinary court process typically allows.

How long does a commercial dispute typically take before the commercial court?

The duration varies based on the dispute's complexity and the volume of evidence required, and we give a realistic estimate after reviewing your specific file rather than a general figure.

Can settlement still be pursued after a lawsuit is filed?

Yes. Settlement remains available at any stage of litigation, and we pursue it in parallel with court proceedings when it serves the client's interest.

What happens if the other party is based outside Saudi Arabia?

Notification procedures and judgment enforcement differ in this case, and we review that specifically as part of assessing the legal position before starting proceedings.

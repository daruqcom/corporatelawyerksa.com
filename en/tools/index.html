Legal Tools & Calculators

Free legal calculators and tools.

This page offers free calculators to estimate figures that come up often in Saudi corporate and employment matters, from end-of-service benefits to zakat and court fees, without waiting for a formal calculation from an accountant or lawyer.

Each tool is built on currently published Saudi regulations and reviewed periodically to ensure it stays aligned with any regulatory update. These tools run entirely within your browser without sending your figures to any server, since the inputs used often include sensitive salary figures or valuations.

Results from these tools are a starting point for planning, not a final figure to rely on in a formal submission, contract, or dispute. For any figure you plan to rely on officially, we recommend confirming the calculation directly with our team, since edge cases in the regulations aren't captured by a general calculator built around the typical scenario.

These tools are often used alongside our related legal services, since these figures typically come up as part of a larger matter, like a labor dispute or tax review, not in isolation from it.

If the figure you need isn't covered by one of these tools, reach out to us directly. Many calculations we run for clients aren't general enough to become a public tool, but we can calculate them for your specific situation.

The end-of-service calculator is often used alongside the zakat calculator when assessing a company's full upcoming financial obligations, rather than as two entirely separate tools.

Common Questions

Are these calculators official or issued by a government body?

No, they're independent estimation tools built on publicly available Saudi regulations, for planning purposes only.

Do you keep the numbers entered into the calculator?

No, the calculation runs entirely in your browser, and we don't retain these figures unless you choose to share them with us directly.

Can the exact figure for my situation be confirmed after using the calculator?

Yes, our team can confirm this directly during a consultation, especially if the figure will be used in a formal submission or contract.
